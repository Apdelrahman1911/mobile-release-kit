### MRK-005 — Medium — IPA, archive, and dSYMs are not correlated

- **Affected:** `src/mobile_release/preflight.py:1100-1155`; `src/mobile_release/cli.py:589-617,698-725`; `.github/workflows/reusable-candidate.yml:950-1008`.
- **Trigger:** Supply an independently valid IPA and archive from different builds.
- **Expected:** Mach-O identities and UUIDs prove the IPA was exported from the retained archive and matches its dSYMs.
- **Actual:** The artifacts are validated and hashed independently; the Store job revalidates only the IPA.
- **Evidence:** Synthetic archive binary `ARCHIVE-BINARY-A` and IPA binary `DIFFERENT-IPA-BINARY-B` both passed.
- **Impact:** Retained archives and symbols may not correspond to the shipped binary.
- **Correction:** Compare main and nested bundle inventories, executable UUIDs, identities, and dSYM UUIDs across archive and IPA.
- **Verification:** Reject archive, IPA, and dSYM substitution independently.
- **Blocks:** iOS candidate provenance and downstream symbol assurance.
