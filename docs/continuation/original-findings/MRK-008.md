### MRK-008 — Medium — Standard macOS private paths are rejected

- **Affected:** `src/mobile_release/tooling.py:29-50`; `src/mobile_release/credentials.py:562-592`.
- **Trigger:** Private material is stored under a normal `/var/folders/...` or `/tmp/...` path.
- **Expected:** Safe system-owned aliases to `/private/var` and `/private/tmp` are accepted.
- **Actual:** `_private_path_error` rejects any symlink ancestor, unlike the canonical path helper.
- **Evidence:** A real `/var/folders/...` temporary credential returned “must not traverse a symlink.”
- **Impact:** Local signing and Store preflight can fail for safe, normal macOS temporary paths.
- **Correction:** Reuse `canonical_external_path` and retain file mode, size, regular-file, and outside-repository checks.
- **Verification:** Accept system aliases but reject lower, user-controlled symlinks.
- **Blocks:** macOS consumer integration and local preflight.
