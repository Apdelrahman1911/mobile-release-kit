### MRK-002 — High — Store mutation occurs before durable evidence

- **Affected:** `src/mobile_release/stores.py:263-329`; `src/mobile_release/cli.py:687-750,783-800`; `fastlane/Fastfile:385-413,493-571`; candidate workflow `482-580,971-1055`; production workflow `240-305`.
- **Trigger:** Cancellation or failure after Store success but before manifest creation, attestation, or artifact upload.
- **Expected:** A retry should recover the exact successful mutation using durable pre-mutation intent and Store reconciliation.
- **Actual:** Evidence is created and uploaded after mutation. Candidate lanes reject an already-existing build, and Android production rejects an existing target version.
- **Evidence:** A mocked Store mutation followed by forced manifest failure produced `store_mutation_happened=True` with no candidate manifest or receipt.
- **Impact:** Successful uploads or drafts can become unauthorizable, forcing manual recovery or a new build number. Ambiguous network failures cannot safely be retried.
- **Cause:** No durable pre-mutation journal or idempotent candidate-adoption protocol exists.
- **Correction:** Attest and retain a pre-mutation intent containing artifact hashes first; implement readback-first reconciliation and exact adoption after ambiguous outcomes.
- **Verification:** Inject failures after each mutation/readback/write/attestation/upload boundary and prove recovery without rebuilding.
- **Blocks:** Toolkit release, candidate creation, promotion recovery, and production preparation.
